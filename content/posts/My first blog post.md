## March 2026

This is a small test entry to see if everything works.

The idea is simple: create a place to log the things I do and think about — climbing sessions, bikepacking trips, slackline days, woodworking projects, and random thoughts that come up along the way.

For a while I’ve felt like platforms like Instagram turn experiences into something performative. Everything becomes a highlight or a small piece of content to share. I wanted something slower.

Something closer to a journal.

So this site is an experiment. A place where I can write without worrying about algorithms or engagement. Just a collection of notes from things I’m doing and learning.

Maybe entries here will look like:

- a climbing day in Frankenjura
- a bikepacking weekend out of Berlin
- the first time I step onto a highline somewhere
- building something in wood that almost works
- reflections after a trip

Some posts will be long.
Some will just be a photo and a few sentences.

The only rule is that they document something real.

For now, this is simply the first test:

March 2026, trying to set up a small corner of the internet.
