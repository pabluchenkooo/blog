Daniel asked me to help with the kitchen in his flat. The room is not so big: about 3.1 m long and 2 m wide, with a counter along one wall, a door that swings into the room and a window at the end. Everything has to fit: the oven he already has, a new fridge, a small dishwasher (60 cm), a washing machine (70 cm) and the Sage Barista Express.

![wide|The plan: plywood everywhere, a dark linoleum countertop](static/images/posts/making/woodworking/kitchen/from_door.jpg)

## From SketchUp to something I can change

I had measured the room and started with SketchUp, but I didn't want the design to live in a cloud app. So I moved the measurements into [OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/), where the kitchen is a small program: the cabinets along the counter are a list (drawers 38, oven 60, sink 60, dishwasher 60, washer 70 cm) and changing a number moves everything after it.

![openscad_plan.jpg](static/images/posts/making/woodworking/kitchen/openscad_plan.jpg)
![openscad_3d.jpg](static/images/posts/making/woodworking/kitchen/openscad_3d.jpg)

## The opposite wall

The first version had a full pantry on the wall opposite the counter. It worked, but it made the room feel even smaller. The final version keeps that wall light: the fridge, a low sideboard with two floating shelves above it, and a floor-to-ceiling shelf behind the door, in the corner that is otherwise dead space. The references were light oak and Japanese pantry shelving; in the end everything is birch plywood.
